# CUBAG app: broker test (30 minutes per person)

**Who:** 3 to 5 members. At least two should be over 50, and at least one should rarely use apps.
**What you need:** their own phone with the CUBAG app installed and signed in, a quiet room, and this sheet.
**Your job:** watch and write down. Do not help unless they are stuck for more than a minute. If you help, write down where.

Before you start, say: *"We are testing the app, not you. If something is hard, that is the app's fault. Please think out loud."*

---

## Tasks

For each task, read the words in quotes, then watch. Tick one box.

| # | Say to the broker | Done alone | Done with help | Could not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Find out when your licence ends." | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 2 | "Show me how you would pay this year's dues." *(stop before they pay)* | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 3 | "Find the receipt for your last payment." | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 4 | "Open your licence certificate." | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 5 | "Find the next CUBAG event and add it to your calendar." | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 6 | "Send CUBAG a question: ask how to change your company address." | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 7 | "Tell CUBAG one thing in the app that is hard to use." *(bottom of Home, or Menu)* | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 8 | "Make the writing in the app bigger." *(phone settings; the app follows)* | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

## Watch for

- Where they **tap first** on each task (write it down, even if it was right).
- Words they **read out loud and question** ("What is 'compliance'?").
- Anything they **cannot read** without bringing the phone close or putting on glasses.
- Any **error message** or screen that looks stuck. Take a photo of the screen.
- Moments they **sigh, laugh or give up**.

## Three questions at the end

1. "What was the hardest part?"
2. "Is there anything you expected to find but did not?"
3. "Would you pay your dues in this app next year instead of at the office? Why or why not?"

---

## After the test

Send the notes and photos to whoever maintains the app. The most useful format is one line per problem:

> **Task 3, broker 2 (age 61):** tapped Payments, then Menu, then found Receipts on Home after 2 minutes. Said "I didn't know Receipts was here."

Feedback sent through *Tell us what is hard* arrives in **Admin → Tickets** with a subject starting **"App feedback:"**. Search the Tickets list for that phrase to see all of it.
